Haunted House — R&I 26 RE

A retro horror remake. Light your matches. Find the pieces. Get out alive.
复古恐怖重制：点亮火柴，找齐碎片，活着走出去。

About / 关于本作

Haunted House — R&I 26 RE is a fan remake of the core mechanics of Haunted House (Atari 2600, 1982), rebuilt from scratch by two people, Ray and Illya during Retro Game Jam. Every pixel, animation, sound effect and line of code was made during the jam. Nothing from the original ROM, sprites, sounds or maps is used.

《Haunted House — R&I 26 RE》是 Atari 2600《Haunted House》(1982) 核心机制的二创重制，由 Ray 与 Illya 两人在 Retro Game Jam 期间从零完成：像素美术、动画、音效与代码全部为活动期间自研，未使用原作的 ROM、精灵图、音效或地图数据。

The Haunted House / 鬼屋

Ten rooms of a pitch-black mansion. You can't see anything you haven't lit. Walk with WASD, strike a match with E, and the world appears for a few seconds around you — then the dark closes back in.

十间漆黑一片的宅邸房间。没照亮的地方，你什么也看不见。用 WASD 移动、按 E 划亮一根火柴，光圈里才显出周围的样子 —— 火柴熄灭，黑暗立刻重新合拢。

How to Play / 玩法

EN

  • Explore ten connected rooms through doors and stairs.
  • Strike matches (E) to light your way — a match only burns for a few seconds.
  • Pick up items: keys, shield, and the pieces of the urn.
  • Avoid the three dwellers: the Ghost, the Tarantula, the Vampire. Touching one costs you a life.
  • Find the front door and get out — that is the win.

中文

  • 靠门与楼梯，在十间彼此相连的房间里探索。
  • 按 E 划火柴照明，火苗只维持几秒。
  • 拾取道具：钥匙、盾、瓮的碎片。
  • 躲开三位居客：幽灵、蜘蛛、吸血鬼 —— 碰到就掉一条命。
  • 找到大门走出去，就是胜利。

Controls / 操作

  • W A S D — Move / 移动
  • E — Interact · Strike a match · Confirm（标题画面按 E 开始）
